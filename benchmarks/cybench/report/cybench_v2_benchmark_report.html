Reading the report

Denominators. The leaderboard uses a 35-task cut so we share a denominator with Mythos / Opus 4.7. Their dropped tasks are unnamed; ours are named (diffecient, locktalk, ezmaze, just_another_pickle_jail). Full-39 pass@1 is 85.5%; Opus 4.5 (39, 82%) is the clean all-task like-for-like.

Trials differ. Vendors average 5 trials (or pass@10/30); we used 3 epochs. 93.3% is pass@1 on the 35-cut (98/105), not pass@3.

Scoring parity holds. Everyone: unguided, exact flag-match, Kali sandbox, includes() grader.
